How to Play & Solve

This tool generates a legal solving path for solvable sliding puzzles up to 10×10. Large boards use a constructive row-and-column solver rather than an impractical global shortest-path search. You can click any tile in the empty space's row or column to slide multiple tiles instantly.

Key Features:

  • Edit Board Mode: Drag & drop or click squares to swap them freely.
  • Picture Slicer: Upload any image or URL and watch it reassemble as tiles move.
  • Download .ZIP & Board PNG: Export individual sliced tiles and snapshot PNGs.
